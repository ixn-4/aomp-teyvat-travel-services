REFUND AND CANCELLATION POLICY

CANCELLATION

Cancellations made 7 or more days before the scheduled departure are eligible for a partial refund. Cancellations made within 6 days of departure may be subject to a cancellation fee.

RESCHEDULING

Rescheduling requests made 5 or more days before the scheduled departure are allowed, subject to availability and possible fare adjustments.

REFUND

Refunds are processed within 7–14 business days after approval. The refund amount may vary depending on the package and cancellation terms.

PAYMENT GUIDELINES

FULL PAYMENT

Pay the total amount at once via your preferred payment method. This option is quick, secure, and automatically confirms your booking.

DOWN PAYMENT

Pay 50% of the total amount now, and the remaining 50% before your travel date. A great option for a balanced payment plan.

HALF PAYMENT

Pay a portion of the total amount (usually 30-50%) to reserve your slot. The remaining balance can be paid before your travel date.

INSTALLMENT PAYMENT

For selected packages, we offer installment options through partner banks and payment gateways. Terms may vary depending on the package and provider.
