TRAVEL GUIDE

Vibe & Atmosphere

A journey to Snezhnaya promises an experience of sub-zero luxury, opulent ice palaces, and cozy nights indoors illuminated by the magnificent aurora borealis.

Wardrobe & Essential Gear

Surviving the freezing climate requires packing multiple thick layers, including a heavy-duty thermal winter coat, insulated snow pants, and fleece-lined waterproof boots. You must also pack thick winter accessories like a traditional ushanka hat, along with reusable hand warmers, a thermal flask for hot drinks, and polarized snow goggles to prevent snow-blindness.
