TRAVEL GUIDE

Vibe & Atmosphere

Your journey through Inazuma will involve beautiful island hopping, humid coastal air, sudden thunderstorms, and culturally immersive shrine visits.

Wardrobe & Essential Gear

You should pack modest, comfortable clothing that allows for easy movement, along with practical slip-on shoes that you can quickly remove when entering sacred shrines. Because the Electro storms are incredibly intense, it is vital to bring a high-quality static-resistant raincoat, rubber-soled boots to keep you grounded, a sturdy camera strap, and a small coin purse for traditional offerings.
