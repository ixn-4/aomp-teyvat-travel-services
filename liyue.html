TRAVEL GUIDE

Vibe & Atmosphere

Liyue Harbor offers a dynamic mix of bustling city markets and steep mountainous terrain, requiring you to prepare for both business engagements and breathtaking mountain climbs.

Wardrobe & Essential Gear

You should bring breathable, flexible clothing, including comfortable streetwear for dining around the lively harbor and a sturdy pair of hiking boots for your journey up to Mt. Aocang. We also suggest packing a light evening jacket for the chilly high altitudes, a secure Mora pouch for world-class shopping, a collapsible walking stick, and a hearty appetite for takeout.
