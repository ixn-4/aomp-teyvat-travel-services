Teyvat Travel Services was founded by Mr. Zhongli, a former traveler who once wandered through the beauty of the seven nations of Teyvat. Inspired by his experiences, he envisioned himself as a beacon of light for others, allowing them to experience and witness the mesmerizing landscapes, cultures, and sceneries that Teyvat has to offer.

Ever since Teyvat Travel Services was established, it has become one of the trusted travel agencies in the land of Teyvat. Although the agency has just started, our mission to provide excellent and accommodating services and our vision to continuously improve our services to meet our clients' needs are the foundations of a growing legacy that will reach future generations.

PAYMENT GUIDELINES

FULL PAYMENT

Pay the total amount at once via your preferred payment method. This option is quick, secure, and automatically confirms your booking.

HALF PAYMENT

Pay a portion of the total amount (usually 30-50%) to reserve your slot. The remaining balance can be paid before your travel date.

DOWN PAYMENT

Pay 50% of the total amount now, and the remaining 50% before your travel date. A great option for a balanced payment plan.

INSTALLMENT PAYMENT

For selected packages, we offer installment options through partner banks and payment gateways. Terms may vary depending on the package and provider.
