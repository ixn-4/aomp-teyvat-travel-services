FEATURED ITINERARIES

A pristine freshwater lake with a vast, crystal-clear surface that has remained unchanged for countless years. Its waters are so clear that the lakebed can be seen beneath the surface, while its cool and refreshing taste provides the perfect source for Mondstadt's renowned ciders and wines. Today, Cider Lake stands as one of the most recognizable symbols of Mondstadt.

Windrise is a scenic destination in the Galesong Hill area of Mondstadt, known for its peaceful surroundings and magnificent oak tree. According to local legends, the site is closely associated with Vennessa, a celebrated figure in Mondstadt's history who is believed to have ascended to Celestia. Today, the towering oak tree and its surrounding landscape make Windrise a memorable destination, offering travelers a peaceful retreat surrounded by nature.

The Favonius Cathedral, also known as the Mondstadt Cathedral, is a prominent landmark located in the heart of Mondstadt City. It is maintained by the Church of Favonius and serves as a place of worship dedicated to Barbatos, the Anemo Archon and God of Freedom. With its grand architecture and peaceful surroundings, the cathedral is an important part of Mondstadt's cultural and spiritual heritage. Visitors can admire its impressive structure while experiencing a quieter side of the lively City of Freedom.

TRAVEL GUIDE

Vibe & Atmosphere

Welcome to Mondstadt, where you will experience breezy days, rolling hills, and a relaxed countryside atmosphere that is absolutely perfect for walking and picnicking.

Wardrobe & Essential Gear

When preparing your wardrobe, we highly recommend packing light layers and a trusty windbreaker to comfortably handle the constant Anemo breezes near Cider Lake and Windrise. You will also want to bring comfortable, flat walking shoes for climbing the endless flights of stairs leading up to the grand Cathedral, along with a picnic blanket, your Wind Glider license, and a reusable water bottle.
