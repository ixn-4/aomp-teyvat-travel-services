TRAVEL GUIDE

Vibe & Atmosphere

Prepare for an athletic and energetic adventure in Natlan, which is a constantly warm nation defined by thermal baths, tribal beats, and intense volcanic heat.

Wardrobe & Essential Gear

Activewear is the standard uniform here, so you must pack breathable shorts, sports tops, and heat-resistant footwear that will keep you comfortable while walking on volcanic rock. You will also need to bring multiple swimsuits for the therapeutic thermal baths, heavy-duty sunscreen, an insulated water flask for constant hydration, and a quick-dry towel.
