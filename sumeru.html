TRAVEL GUIDE

01 - Vibe & Atmosphere

Sumeru offers a unique and breathtaking blend of vibrant academic campus life and deep-jungle exploration within a warm, incredibly humid rainforest environment.

02 - Wardrobe & Essential Gear

Moisture-wicking fabrics will be your best friend here, so we recommend a safari-chic wardrobe featuring lightweight cargo pants, breathable linen shirts, and a wide-brimmed sun hat. To navigate the region comfortably, you must also pack heavy-duty bug spray, a durable waterproof backpack for sudden downpours, and a physical notebook and pen for taking notes at the Akademiya.

YOUR DAY-BAG CHECKLIST

  • Comfortable clothes
  • Pen
  • Bug spray
  • Waterproof Backpack
  • Notebook
