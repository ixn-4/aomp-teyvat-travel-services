TRAVEL GUIDE

01 - Vibe & Atmosphere

Welcome to Mondstadt, where you will experience breezy days, rolling hills, and a relaxing countryside atmosphere—perfect for leisurely walks and peaceful picnics. Take your time to unwind and admire your surroundings while remaining mindful of your safety. From the gentle breeze to the lush greenery, every little detail can leave a lasting memory and bring comfort and joy to your heart!

02 - Wardrobe & Essential Gear

When preparing your wardrobe, we highly recommend packing light layers and a trusty windbreaker to comfortably handle the constant Anemo breezes near Cider Lake and Windrise. You will also want to bring comfortable, flat walking shoes for climbing the endless flights of stairs leading up to the grand Cathedral, along with a picnic blanket, your Wind Glider license, and a reusable water bottle.

YOUR DAY-BAG CHECKLIST

  • Water bottle
  • Walking shoes
  • Personal medications
  • Picnic blanket
  • Light layers
  • Foods and Beverages
  • Windbreaker
  • Wind Glider license
